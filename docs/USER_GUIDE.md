# User Guide (Cashier & Kitchen Staff)

## Logging In
Go to the app URL, enter the email and password given to you by your manager. If you forget your password, ask an Admin or Manager to reset it via Staff Management — there's no self-service reset yet.

## Clocking In/Out
Click **Clock In** in the top-right corner when your shift starts. It turns into a green **On Shift** button — click it again to clock out at the end of your shift.

## Taking an Order (Cashier)
1. Go to **New Order**.
2. Tap menu items to add them to the cart on the right; tap again (or use +/-) to adjust quantity.
3. Choose **Takeaway** or **Dine In**, and enter a table number or pickup token if relevant.
4. Tap **Place Order** — it's sent to the kitchen immediately.
5. Once ready, tap **Take Payment**, choose Cash/Card/Online, and confirm. You can then **View Receipt** to print or show the customer.

## Working the Kitchen Display (Kitchen Staff)
- New orders appear automatically in the **New Orders** column — no refresh needed.
- Tap **Start Preparing** to move an order into the **Preparing** column, then **Mark Ready** when it's done.
- Tap **Complete Order** once it's handed off — this is what updates inventory and sales reports.
- If an order needs to be cancelled, use the red X icon (you'll be asked to confirm).

## Checking Inventory (Kitchen Staff)
Go to **Inventory** to see current stock levels. You can view but not edit — if something's running low, tell your manager, or they'll see the same low-stock alert automatically.
